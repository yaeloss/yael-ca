The legal system shapes the prices consumers pay and the products businesses can offer. My work examines litigation finance, legal advertising, class actions, and the incentives behind high-profile lawsuits.

These articles explore how legal reform can protect access to justice while addressing costs and unintended consequences for consumers. See my [biography](/about/) for more about my policy work.
